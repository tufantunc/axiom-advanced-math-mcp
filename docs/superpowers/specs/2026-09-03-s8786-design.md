## The borderline case: the degree pattern

This is the deliberate post-DoS-fix form (`\d+(?:\.\d*)?` — the 208s form
was `\d+\.?\d*`, fixed before this branch; the fix and its measurement are
in the performance pack). A single-path rewrite was attempted and
empirically rejected: `\d+(?:\.\d+)?\.?` measured SLOWER (184ms) and
differed on 132 of 20,000 fuzz inputs (the greedy leading `\d+` captures
more). The quadratic character is structural to the match shape; possessive
quantifiers would remove it but require Node 22+ (engines floor is 20).
Under the 8KB cap the pattern stays at ~100–160ms worst case — under the
pack's High bar by an order of magnitude.

## Decision

No code changes. All 11 Sonar findings are marked wontfix with the measured
rationale (the same treatment as S7778's section boundaries). The standing
watch note stays: if MAX_EXPRESSION_LENGTH is ever raised (32KB → ~1.7s),
re-measure the degree pair before shipping that change.
